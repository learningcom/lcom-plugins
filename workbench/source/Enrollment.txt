Enrollment

Enrollment data are available from LCom Platform populated by teachers and district cordinators and from
NCES (https://nces.ed.gov/, a federal agency within the Department of Education that compiles and standardizes data from all public schools, 
private schools, and universities in the United States). NCES data are available in Salesforce.

Both sources are not 100% accurate due to different reasons.

LCom Platform Enrollment may include students and schools not using LCom Platform at all. Students leaving a school or districts are not excluded from the roster.

NCES data can be outdated, summary of school enrollment are not always equal district enrollment.
